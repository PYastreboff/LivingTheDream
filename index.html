The Workout Vending Machine

Scan. Pick. Train.

No plan? No problem. Scan the QR code on the poster in the gym, pick what you want to train, and the machine vends you a random workout I've written. One roll a day, so make it count.

  1. Scan the code on the poster with your phone camera.
  2. Pick a category — push, pull, legs, full body, or roll the dice on random.
  3. Get after it. Tick off each exercise as you go, then come back tomorrow for another roll.
